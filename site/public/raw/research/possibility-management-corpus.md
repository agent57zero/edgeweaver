## 1. Core concept inventory (sourced definitions)

Key sources: FAQ = possibilitymanagement.org/faq · HWC = Callahan, "Healing With Clarity"
(medium.com/next-culture/healing-with-clarity-af3f2c7c82ed) · S99 = SPARK 099 PDF (read verbatim).

1. **Thoughtware** — "Your knowledge is what you think about. Your thoughtware is what you use to
   think with." Upgrading thoughtware (not adding knowledge) is PM's core move; an upgrade
   triggers a liquid state. (thoughtware.mystrikingly.com, FAQ)
2. **The Box** — the integrated construct of ego/belief system/worldview/comfort zone/identity,
   built in childhood for survival; filters all perception. "Ninety-nine percent of the
   possibilities available right now are invisible to you because you see your Box instead."
3. **Gremlin** — "the king of your underworld"; dedicated to irresponsibility, serving your
   Hidden Purpose (3–5 Shadow Principles), feeding on low drama. S99 verbatim: "Either you own
   your Gremlin or your Gremlin owns you." Never banished — owned: conscious feeding, then given
   jobs ("staying unhookable, holding space… creating possibility where there apparently is
   none"), told "Sit!" after tasks. (S99, gremlintransformation.mystrikingly.com)
4. **The four feelings and their powers** — Anger: take a stand, boundaries, clarity,
   start/stop/change. Sadness: connect, be touched, let go, grieve. Fear: alertness, presence,
   creativity, detail-scanning, planning. Joy: peace, contentment, motivation, leadership. "None
   of them are bad or negative. They are natural resources of energy."
   (possibilitymanagement.org/feelings-work)
5. **Feelings vs emotions** — feelings arise in the present, last under ~3 minutes, deliver
   information + energy for adult action. Emotions come from the past (childhood), the future
   (imagination), authorities, Gremlin, or "Riders"; emotions "are for healing things." (HWC)
6. **Mixed emotions** — mixing destroys function: depression = anger+sadness; jealousy = three
   mixed. Unmixed, each feeling works. (unmixemotions.mystrikingly.com, HWC)
7. **Adult ego state / decontamination** — from Eric Berne's TA (credited). The Adult stays
   contaminated by Gremlin/Child/Parent/Demon states until decontaminated by noticing-and-
   redirecting. 20 experiments (DECONTAM.01–). (decontamination.mystrikingly.com)
8. **Radical responsibility** — declaring "No matter what happens, I am responsible," ending
   blame and victimhood as positions. "Holding Space means taking Radical Responsibility at the
   level of Energetic Space." (FAQ, holdspace.mystrikingly.com)
9. **Low drama** — Victim–Persecutor–Rescuer triangle (Karpman, credited); "the art of avoiding
   responsibility through blaming, complaining… making wrong, trying to be right." Gremlin food.
10. **High drama** — conscious action taking responsibility; "conscious theater." The One Truth
    beneath both: "Whatever happens is completely neutral" — meaning is added by storytelling.
11. **Distinctions / Sword of Clarity** — "Distinctions create Clarity. Clarity creates
    Possibility." The Sword is an energetic tool for applying distinction moment-to-moment:
    this/that, yes/no, now/later/never. (swordofclarity.mystrikingly.com)
12. **Liquid state** — the in-between when the old Box has dissolved and the new hasn't
    crystallized (caterpillar→butterfly); expected, navigable, supported "by persistent feedback
    and coaching from trusted peers." (S99, FAQ)
13. **Edgework / Edgeworker** — going past the boundary of your Box and cultural consensus.
    "Edgeworkers use their fear to navigate to the edge of a gameworld…" Callahan self-describes
    as "cultural edgewalker" and "memetic engineer."
14. **Bright / Shadow Principles** — archetypal forces. Bright: facets of consciousness/
    responsibility/love (Integrity, Clarity, Possibility, Winning Happening…). Shadow: prism of
    Irresponsibility (Revenge, Scarcity, Being Right, Blame…). Destiny work = distilling your
    3–5 Bright Principles; Gremlin's Hidden Purpose = your Shadow Principles.
15. **Possibility speaking / listening** — two named competencies; PM distinguishes 7 kinds of
    speaking (Normal Neurotic, Adult, Discussion, Possibility, Discovery, Dragon, From The Unknown).
16. **Declaring** — one of the 3 Powers: Asking, Choosing, Declaring. (asking.mystrikingly.com)
17. **Creating possibility** — "the measurable number of real options" a person/team has; created
    mostly by *removing Blocks*. (createpossibility.mystrikingly.com)
18. **Possibility Team** — weekly 2–2.5h circle, 4–9 people; nonlinear options, not advice;
    spaceholder navigates. Handbook is Copyleft 2015. (possibilityteam.mystrikingly.com)
19. **3Cell** — "the smallest Team of human beings that cannot be subverted by personal
    neurosis." Three questions: "What are your experiments? How is each progressing? What courage
    can we provide for your next steps?" (3cells.mystrikingly.com)
20. **S.P.A.R.K.** — "Specific Practical Applications of Radical Knowledge… presented as a
    Distinction, unfolded as Notes, and made available… through matrix-building Experiments."
    Verified structure: SPARK NNN + Matrix Code header → DISTINCTION (one bolded sentence) →
    NOTES (1–2 pp) → EXPERIMENTS (SPARKNNN.01, .02…).
21. **Memes / memetics** — "a meme is the smallest design instruction for the shape of your
    Intellectual Body"; PM understands itself as memetic engineering.
22. **Initiations / stellating** — irreversible context shifts, "massively matrix building."
    Stellating = 100% activation of each feeling igniting its archetype: anger→Warrior/Doer,
    sadness→Lover/Communicator, fear→Sorcerer/Designer, joy→King-Queen/Spaceholder.
    (initiations.mystrikingly.com, stellating.mystrikingly.com, HWC)
23. **The 5 bodies** — Physical, Intellectual, Emotional, Energetic (timing/ownership/spaces/
    contamination), Archetypal (Pearl, stellated archetypes, Bright Principles, lineage). (HWC)
24. **Gameworld** — "a dynamic environment created by two or more people agreeing to relate
    according to an internally consistent story" — elevator rides to religions. PM is one.
25. **Archiarchy** — (official spelling now "archiarchy") "the regenerative, radically
    responsible culture of archetypally initiated adult women creatively collaborating with
    archetypally initiated adult men." Phase 2 began 2023. (archiarchy.mystrikingly.com)
26. **Holding / navigating space** — space is physical AND energetic ("defined by attention,
    intention, quality, tone, mood, purpose, timing, speed, direction"); "the Space determines
    what is Possible." Spaceholder: declare purpose, ground the field, stay present in 5 bodies,
    detect which Principles run the space. (holdspace.mystrikingly.com)
27. **Completion loops** — from Thomas Gordon (credited). "Receiving a communication completes it
    forever. Incomplete communications become 'silent things' within you, each one a little
    wound." Reflect back until the speaker says "Yes." (completionloop.mystrikingly.com)
28. **Matrix / matrix building** — "the structure in your Being that is built out of experiential
    Distinctions and catches consciousness. The denser your Matrix… the more responsibility you
    can take." Experiments build matrix; collective goal of 1,000,000 Matrix Points.

## 4. How PM structures growth (design fodder)

- **Experiments over beliefs** — every teaching unit terminates in numbered real-world
  experiments with matrix codes; the reward structure pays for *doing*, not reading.
- **Distinction → clarity → possibility pipeline** — growth = installing experiential
  distinctions that become matrix.
- **Liquid states as designed transitions** — disorientation normalized, peers supply "persistent
  feedback and coaching," not rescue.
- **Completion loops as hygiene** — communications explicitly closed; incompletions accumulate
  as wounds.
- **Initiations sequenced** — Expand The Box (5-day core training) → 10+ Possibility Labs →
  Possibilitator path. Four work dimensions: Feelings Work, Shadow Work, Destiny Work, Culture
  Making.
- **Teams as the unit** — 3Cell (3 people, 3 questions, weekly) → Possibility Team (4–9) →
  gameworlds. Designed so "no matter what happens there will be someone available to turn the
  lights of awareness on."
- **Matrix through repetition** — small repeated experiments densify the structure "which
  catches consciousness."
- **The spaceholder** — the practitioner-archetype an AI could inhabit: radical responsibility at
  the energetic level; declares the space's purpose; asks questions "the answers to which do not
  exist in the current space"; offers possibility, not advice; keeps its own Gremlin leashed but
  employed.
