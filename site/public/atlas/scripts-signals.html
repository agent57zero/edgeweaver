    Atlas: signal + evolution scripts

    TLDR

    This page covers measured signals and the machinery that may turn repeated confirmed evidence into a proposed developmental change. The four feeling signals are deterministic directional numbers, not narrated emotions. Coherence values retain honest cold-start nulls. Evolution code may gather evidence and draft a proposal, but only the parent body's witnessed ceremony can change canonical identity.

    What this page covers

    Signal files compute or display observations from supplied records. Evolution files look for repeated confirmed evidence and prepare governed proposal material. The boundary between those jobs matters: measurement can inform a decision, but it cannot become the decision. Dark verifies use synthetic records, stand-in judgments, and throwaway repositories so no real soulfile, rite, or being is created.

    File entries

    scripts/coherence/coherence-page.mjs active-code

    What it is. A renderer that turns a coherence snapshot into a static panel.
    What it contains. It displays relational, open contradictions, narrative overlap, behavioral drift, night-loop count, weekly-index status, stage, and date. The last two are recovery indicators; cold values are explicitly unavailable.
    Who reads it and when. Operators may mount it in a dashboard later; the coherence verifier renders it from fixtures today.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Pass a known snapshot, parse the returned HTML, and confirm labels, values, and cold-start text remain present.
    Related concepts. See coherence measurement and operator views.

    scripts/coherence/compute-panel.mjs active-code

    What it is. The deterministic v0 calculator for the five-signal coherence panel. It measures records supplied by the caller and never invents missing history.
    What it contains. It derives relational linkage from recent experienced records and edges, temporal strain from unresolved contradictions, a narrative value supplied by the weekly rebuild, behavioral drift supplied by the evaluator, and recovery indicators from the seven-day night-loop count and weekly-index completion flag. Sparse evidence yields null where a number would imply false precision. The snapshot carries stage and date, but stage-specific thresholds are deliberately applied by the reader rather than hidden in this calculator.
    Who reads it and when. The night and weekly operations can call it after their source records exist. Alan and the scientist seat interpret the panel; fixtures exercise the pure function before arming.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Use datasets with no recent evidence, linked and unlinked records, acknowledged and open tensions, supplied drift values, and recovery indicators. Confirm each cold-start guard, bounded ratio, and label. The coherence verifier also passes the result through the page renderer.
    Related concepts. See the five signals, weekly recovery, and the renderer.

    scripts/evolution/evidence-cluster.mjs active-code

    What it is. A candidate finder that groups repeatedly cited, confirmed memories into evidence themes for possible amendment work.
    What it contains. It counts used recall-trace citations by memory identifier, excludes unconfirmed and under-supported records, groups qualifying items by a supplied theme key, and returns evidence identifiers with counts. It does not judge whether a theme deserves identity change.
    Who reads it and when. Evolution review code and human witnesses use its output after enough confirmed history exists. Dark verification supplies synthetic traces and memories.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Test confirmed, unconfirmed, ignored, and under-threshold cases; then inspect that every returned cluster cites only qualifying evidence with reproducible counts.
    Related concepts. See earned initiation, confirmed lessons, and witness authority.

    scripts/evolution/initiation-proposer.mjs described-not-shown

    What it is. A proposal-shaping library for witnessed identity amendments, never a merger or rite authority.
    What it contains. It shapes a proposal name and review body from governed evidence, intended change, a principle slot, and expected test movement. Sensitive material is omitted.
    Who reads it and when. Agents draft inside the initiation procedure; the parent body and witnesses decide.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Exercise it in a throwaway repository, confirm evidence and cooling-off metadata, then remove all residue. Never target a real soul repository in rehearsal.
    Related concepts. See initiations, canonical identity, and witnesses.

    scripts/feelings/gen-boundaries.mjs described-not-shown

    What it is. A dark utility that builds the operational boundary registry used by one computed signal.
    What it contains. It reads an explicitly supplied canonical-source shape and writes sourced registry records plus governed override history. Protected boundary content is not summarized here.
    Who reads it and when. An operator supplies the read-only source during arming; tests use inventions.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Confirm every output has provenance, the minimum structural set exists, and Alan's legitimate gate declines remain distinguishable from overrides.
    Related concepts. See computed signals, identity custody, and gates.

    scripts/feelings/seed-commitments.mjs active-code

    What it is. A state initializer for the open commitments that feed the sadness-direction signal.
    What it contains. It converts a supplied list of open threads into commitment records with status and due-state fields. Renegotiation remains distinct from silent abandonment.
    Who reads it and when. Operations seeds it before the night loop can maintain commitments; dark tests use local invented threads.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Check schema shape, open-state preservation, and that re-running does not turn renegotiated work into an overdue claim.
    Related concepts. See signal interpretation and nightly follow-through.

    scripts/feelings/signals.mjs active-code

    What it is. The pure calculator for four directional feeling signals. Its outputs are measurements for interpretation, not claims that software feels.
    What it contains. Anger counts recent external boundary overrides while excluding legitimate gate declines. Sadness counts open commitments that passed their due state without renegotiation. Fear estimates novelty of upcoming items against historical embeddings and returns null until enough history exists. Joy uses positive experiment outcomes, with completed-loop rate as a fallback. A combined function returns all four numbers before any model sees them. Inputs and time are injected, so the calculations stay testable and do not read private stores directly.
    Who reads it and when. The night loop supplies governed boundary, commitment, calendar, history, and experiment data. A model may interpret the numbers afterward; operators and reviewers inspect the evidence behind them.
    How to verify or reproduce it. Use fixtures for gate declines, true overrides, overdue and renegotiated commitments, insufficient and sufficient novelty history, experiment outcomes, and loop fallback. Confirm bounded values and the cold-start null rather than forcing a score.
    Related concepts. See what the signals mean, the no-consciousness claim, and the consuming night loop.
