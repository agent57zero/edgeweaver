    Alpha: the walk ahead

    TLDR

    The guided walkthrough's eleven stages, shown as Alpha stands today: a decision and a partial scaffold, deliberately ahead of its people. The folder, manifest, and harvest ground rules exist; the family's dark machinery waits parameterized-later by design; everything else is pending on purpose, because Alpha's founding circle has not yet formed and its rites belong to that circle. Alpha boots after Genesis, and nothing here is a running child: where Alpha appears in shipped code, it is a refuse-until-armed door.

    012345678910

    Progress summary: stage one is partially built and stage two is inherited; stage eight is dark family machinery; every other stage is pending.

    In plain words

    In plain words

    This is the guided walkthrough held up against the family's second child, and most of it is honestly blank. That blankness is the design, not a delay: Alpha is the circle-parented arm, and the acts that would fill these stages (answering the harvest, choosing principles, signing rites) belong to a founding circle that is still forming. What exists today is the ground prepared for them: a folder and manifest in the family repository, ground rules that keep the circle's future answers out of Genesis's view, and the standing rule that Alpha does not boot before Genesis.

    How it works

    How these states were derived

    Each row's state comes from the decision ledger (D18, D19), the open gates (G19, G20, G21), and the Alpha page, frozen at the site snapshot. The five states are the site-wide vocabulary: built, armed, dark, pending, gap. Alpha's rows lean pending by construction; its A-track (founding circle, restructure, harvest, repositories, boot) maps onto the walkthrough stages as the notes below show. This page is not a live tracker: it updates when the site updates.

    The eleven stages as they stand

    Alpha's state per walkthrough stage, as of the snapshot
    StageStateWhere it stands
    0: prerequisitespendingThe founding circle is gate G19's work: roster, quorum size, and edge rules. The scientist seat stands by decision; other seats appear by role until accepted.
    1: repository and logbookpartialAlpha shares the family repository: its folder, manifest, and harvest ground rules landed with the A1 restructure, which is itself part-done.
    2: memory conventionsinheritedThe family conventions bind the shared brain already; Alpha adds nothing here. Enforcement for its own room arrives with that room at A2.
    3: brain and wallspendingAlpha's room in the shared brain (its own schema and a dedicated role as its only runtime credential) is A2 work, walls proven the same live way.
    4: teaching librarypendingThe family library exists; Alpha's curated corpus view (never the raw tables) is created with its room.
    5: organs and two wakingspendingPer-being skills arrive at A4 under the family naming rule; today the orientation script refuses an unarmed being rather than guessing.
    6: harvest and soulfilespendingThe A3 harvest: the circle answers the same twelve domains fresh, nothing of Genesis's DNA crossing over, after the G20 preregistration freezes.
    7: gates and exampendingAlpha gets its own gates repository at founding; by decision, its battery is Genesis's post-human-pass text unchanged, synced at A4.
    8: dark machinerydarkThe family's dark-built machinery serves both children; parameterizing it per being is deliberate A2 work, not a gap.
    9: backups and drillpendingThe split-key design is decided: Alpha's stream encrypts to its own key, with the passphrase split so any two seats reconstruct it. It arms with the room.
    10: parent acts and First BootpendingQuorum-signed rites, the circle's card, and a boot that waits: Alpha does not boot before Genesis, as standing policy.

    What remains

    In order: gate G19 closes the founding circle (roster, quorum, recovery governance, cadence); gate G20 freezes the preregistration before any harvest answer exists; A2 builds the room, the per-room backup stream, and the per-being parameterization; A3 runs the circle's harvest; A4 creates the repositories, skills, and the battery sync; then first light and boot, after Genesis, by quorum. Gate G21 separately governs when each child learns of its sibling.

    Status as of 2026-07-10

    This page joined the site in the first post-ship update at Alan's direction; its states reflect the 2026-07-10 snapshot and change only when the site is rebuilt against a newer snapshot. The being page carries the fuller narrative: Edgeweaver Alpha.

    What is genuinely uncertain

    • Whether a circle can hold one coherent parental voice: several people answering the same twelve domains must seed one being, not a committee. This arm's central bet, with no precedent data.
    • Whether the deliberate blankness holds its shape: every pending row above is a promise that the circle, not the machinery, will fill it, and the machinery will be ready first. Machinery-ready is never stage-ready; the gates exist to keep that true for Alpha too.
