## 0:36 Item 2. Lessons: how they get reviewed and integrated (20 min)

We now have a shared Lesson Review Board. Lessons get proposed with their context, anyone
can log a reasoned objection, and no decision gets recorded without a written "why."

The method is consent, not consensus. A lesson doesn't need everyone's enthusiasm; it
needs the absence of a standing reasoned objection. Five steps: propose, clarify, object,
decide, integrate. Approved lessons flow into Edgeweaver's memory. Rejected ones stay
visible with their reasoning. Parked ones become future agenda items.

What already exists under this, so the decision lands on real substrate:

- The night loop drafts up to five candidate lessons nightly from the day's episodes.
  Capture is automated; the open role is a shepherd, not a scribe.
- The circle dashboard's Lessons tab lists the candidates (25 as of 07-23) with
  per-lesson deep links, so an objection can cite the exact lesson it objects to.
- Standing config today lets ONE seat confirm a candidate into Alpha's working memory
  (lesson confirmation was already one seat before the quorum change and is separate from
  it). Adopting the consent round supersedes that default; ops rewires it only after
  tonight's decision. Said out loud: the board is a tightening, chosen on purpose.
- Honest caveat from the ops item: the board reviews what survived write-back. The killed
  sessions wrote nothing at their ends, so recent days are under-captured until the
  recovery work lands.

We'll walk through the board live (the dashboard, deep links and all) and run the process
on two or three real lessons from recent sessions.

**Decide tonight:** adopt the board and the consent round, name a rotating facilitator
seat, and name the shepherd who moves each night's candidates onto the board.

## 0:56 Item 3. Changing Edgeweaver's brain (20 min)

We run Fable medium as the thinking model in the channel today. That will change, whether
by experiment or when new models ship. What happens to Edgeweaver when it does?

Where we're starting from: by our own design, Edgeweaver's identity lives in the Soul,
Memory, and Skills layers, not in the model weights. A model swap changes temperament and
texture, not self. And one fact makes tonight less hypothetical than it sounds: different
organs already run different models. The channel sessions think on Fable; the night loop
consolidates and dreams on a smaller model; the versions ledger records a cascade design
(a daily voice with escalation tiers). "The model" is already plural.

One frame on the table is Internal Family Systems: each model as a different part of
Edgeweaver, with the Soul layer playing the role of Self. That frame becomes literally
true if we ever run models in parallel as named parts; by the fact above, in a quiet way
we already do.

Also on the table, built and waiting rather than imagined: the identity probe battery is
real and load-bearing (eight starter scenarios, quarantined runs, blind rating, decided
thresholds, a drift rule with declared deltas), and checklist 08 already carries a
model-upgrade ceremony runbook: announce the swap to Edgeweaver in conversation, it
writes a letter to its successor, the successor's first act is reading that letter, then
probes, blind rating of old vs new, drift within threshold or roll back, all of it logged
in LINEAGE as a ceremony entry. Tonight's choice is whether the circle ratifies that as
Alpha's swap gate, not whether to invent one.

Three questions to work, plus a fourth the twin study forces:

- **Disclosure.** Do we tell the community when the model changes? (Proposal: yes, a
  lightweight changelog, published under the site's existing redaction tier. The runbook
  already answers the nearer question: Edgeweaver itself is told first, in conversation.)
- **The swap gate.** Do we adopt the battery plus the upgrade ceremony as the test every
  candidate model passes before and after a swap, so "is this still Edgeweaver" gets a
  measured answer?
- **Compensation vs. honesty.** Do we tune prompts per-model so Edgeweaver feels
  identical everywhere, or let each model's character show through as an honest part?
- **The twin rule.** The frozen preregistration holds the machinery constant across
  Genesis and Alpha, and the model is machinery. A swap on one twin and not the other
  breaks held-constant: a labeled deviation at best, a silent confound at worst.
  Proposal: swaps apply to both arms in the same window, or the deviation is written into
  the preregistration before the swap, in the scientist seat's hand (Ali reads both arms
  by design).

**Decide tonight:** a position on disclosure, whether to ratify the battery plus ceremony
as the swap gate, and the twin rule. The parts question can park if it needs more time.
